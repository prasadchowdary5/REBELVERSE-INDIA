THE JOURNEY OF PRABHAS

From a young actor to India's Rebel Star.

EARLY LIFE

Uppalapati Venkata Suryanarayana Prabhas Raju was born on October 23, 1979, in Madras (now Chennai). He grew up in a family with deep roots in Telugu cinema.

CAREER BEGINNING

Prabhas made his acting debut in 2002 with "Eeswar." He gained early recognition with films like "Varsham" (2004) and "Chatrapathi" (2005).

REBEL STAR

With "Darling" (2010) and "Mirchi" (2013), Prabhas became one of Telugu cinema's most bankable stars. "Mirchi" earned him the title "Rebel Star."

BAAHUBALI

"Baahubali: The Beginning" (2015) and "Baahubali 2: The Conclusion" (2017) turned Prabhas into a pan-India phenomenon and a global icon.

PAN-INDIA JOURNEY

Following Baahubali, Prabhas starred in "Saaho" (2019), "Radhe Shyam" (2022), "Adipurush" (2023), and "Salaar" (2023), expanding his reach across Indian languages.

RECENT CAREER

In 2024, Prabhas starred in the sci-fi epic "Kalki 2898 AD," further cementing his status as one of India's biggest stars.
